# Pine needles in gutters

If you live near pine, you've probably found that your gutters block faster than your neighbours' on open estates. That's not bad luck. Pine needles behave differently from leaves.

Updated 24 November 2026

## Where this matters locally

Camberley's wooded estates survive from 19th-century pine plantations ([Surrey Heath](https://www.surreyheath.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2023-05/WUASPD%20Adopted.pdf)). Sandhurst is framed by woods, heaths and fir plantations. Hawley has roads named Fir Drive and Birch Drive, and tree-work applications in South Farnborough regularly involve protected pines.

## Why needles are worse than leaves

- **They fall all year**, not in one autumn drop, so a single winter clean doesn't keep them clear.
- **They knit together** into a mat that holds water and silt.
- **They slip through guards.** Needles thread through brush guards and coarse mesh, then lodge.
- **They plug downpipes.** Needles wash into the outlet and compact.

## What works

1. Plan two or three cleans a year rather than one
2. If you want guards, use fine micro-mesh only (see [do gutter guards work?](https://www.gutterteam.co.uk/advice/gutter-guards-do-they-work/))
3. Make sure downpipes are cleared and flow-tested, not just the gutter
4. Ask for photos so you can see the condition inside the gutter
