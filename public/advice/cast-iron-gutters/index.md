# Cast iron gutters on period homes

Farnham's Georgian centre, Fleet's Edwardian streets and South Farnborough's villas still have cast iron gutters on many houses. Looked after, cast iron outlasts plastic by decades. Neglected, it rusts at the joints and leaks.

Updated 24 November 2026

## Why it's worth keeping

Correctly maintained cast iron rainwater goods can last more than 100 years, and traditional half-round and ogee profiles are still made ([North Devon Council conservation guidance](https://www.northdevon.gov.uk/media/305338/georgeham-mp-cmyk.pdf)). Plastic can become brittle in sunlight and rarely lasts as long.

## Routine care

- **Clean once or twice a year.** Standing water and wet debris speed up corrosion.
- **Reseal joints** when they drip. Cast iron joints are bolted and sealed, and old sealant fails.
- **Keep it painted.** Paint is what protects the iron. Repaint when you see rust bloom.

## Listed buildings and conservation areas

Farnham has 387 listed buildings ([Waverley](https://www.waverley.gov.uk/portals/0/documents/services/planning-and-building/planning-strategies-and-policies/farnham%20ca.pdf)). North Fleet is a conservation area with an Article 4 direction. Like-for-like cleaning and repair don't change appearance. Replacing cast iron with plastic, or changing the profile, on a listed building usually needs listed building consent, so check with the council first.

## Not sure it's cast iron?

Cast iron is thin-walled, rings when tapped and a magnet sticks to it. On houses built or refurbished before 2000, thick grey gutters and downpipes that a magnet won't stick to may be asbestos cement ([HSE](https://www.hse.gov.uk/asbestos/diagrams.htm)). Clearing debris from them is routine, but don't drill, cut, scrape or break them. If you plan to replace them, have them tested first. [AsbestosCertified explains asbestos testing](https://www.asbestoscertified.co.uk/asbestos-testing) around Farnborough and Guildford.
