# Signs your gutters need cleaning

You don't need a ladder to tell whether gutters need attention. Most warning signs can be seen from the garden or the pavement, especially during or just after rain.

Updated 24 November 2026

## Check during rain

1. **Water spilling over the front edge.** The most obvious sign. A blockage or a full gutter.
2. **Water pouring next to a downpipe.** Usually a blocked outlet or downpipe rather than a full gutter.
3. **Dripping from joints.** Failing seals, often on systems 25 or more years old.

## Check in dry weather

1. **Plants growing out of the gutter.** There's enough soil in there to grow seedlings.
2. **Sagging runs.** Weight from wet debris, or failed brackets.
3. **Staining on brickwork or fascias** below the gutter line.
4. **Damp patches inside** on upper walls or ceilings near the eaves.

On rendered houses, overflow tends to leave dark streaks down the same patch of wall and can get into cracks behind the render. [Guildford Render explains how render and damp interact](https://www.guildfordrender.co.uk/render-and-damp).

## Don't climb

Falls from height were the most common cause of fatal injury to workers in Great Britain in 2024/25, causing 35 deaths ([HSE](https://www.hse.gov.uk/STATISTICS/fatals-overview.htm)). A ground-level check plus photos from a professional visit is safer.
