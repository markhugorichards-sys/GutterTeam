Key findings

  • October to December is both leaf-fall season and the wettest quarter in southern England, averaging about 86 to 87 mm of rain a month (Met Office, 1991 to 2020).
  • Typical UK gutter cleaning costs £60 to £180, with the South East around 20 to 30% higher.
  • Falls from height caused 35 worker deaths in Great Britain in 2024/25, the most common cause of fatal injury at work.
  • Locally, housing ranges from Farnham's listed Georgian core to large 1960s to 1980s estates, which make up around 80% of Blackwater North.

Methodology

Figures were compiled in October 2026 from primary and official sources where available: the Met Office, HSE, the English Housing Survey, the Association of British Insurers, and council conservation area appraisals and urban character studies for Rushmoor, Hart, Surrey Heath and Waverley. Price figures come from published consumer and trade price guides and are estimates, not official statistics. Regional climate figures are 30-year averages for the Met Office's southern England region rather than a single local station. Local housing figures come from council studies of different dates, noted against each source. They describe character, not current counts.

Limitations

Price guides vary in method and sample, and they aren't independently audited. Council character studies date from 2010 to 2025 and may not reflect every recent development. Where a source is a secondary summary of official data, such as the Centre for Ageing Better's use of the English Housing Survey, it's labelled as such.
