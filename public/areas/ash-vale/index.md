## Which parts of Ash and Ash Vale are covered?

Ash Vale owes its growth to the railway and the garrison. Its station opened in 1870, and Ash station dates from 1849. The village sits between the River Blackwater and the Basingstoke Canal, with the open heathland of Ash Ranges beyond. Victorian cottages near the stations stand alongside post-war and modern estates.

**Ash Vale village**  
Victorian semi-detached houses and cottages close to the station, many with original bay roofs that have their own small gutter runs.

**Ash Wharf**  
Homes around the canal wharf and the busy crossroads by the local shops.

**Ash and Ash Green**  
The older parish south of the canal on London Clay, with St Peter's Church at its centre.

## What are Ash and Ash Vale homes and gutters like?

### Victorian cottages and semis

Built as the railway and garrison brought people in. Look out for bay roofs, slate, and mixed gutter profiles after decades of piecemeal repair.

### Post-war and later estates

Standard two-storey uPVC systems, usually quick to clear from the ground.

## How do Ash and Ash Vale’s trees affect your gutters?

Much of the parish lies on Bagshot Sands, a sandy ridge historically covered by heather and conifer plantations. Homes on the heath side pick up pine needles and birch seed.

The Basingstoke Canal runs through Ash Vale and Ash, crossing the Blackwater on an aqueduct.

### What problems are most common in Ash and Ash Vale?

- Bay-roof gutters on Victorian cottages, which are small and quick to block
- Pine needles and birch seed on homes near the heath
- Mixed gutter profiles after old repairs, which make sourcing matching parts harder

**Best time to book in Ash and Ash Vale:** Clean once each winter. If you're close to the heath and conifers, add a spring check.

## Ash and Ash Vale questions

### Is Ash Vale covered even though it's in Surrey?

Yes. Ash, Ash Vale and Ash Wharf are all inside the coverage area, along with neighbouring Mytchett.

### My bay roof gutter keeps overflowing. Why?

Bay gutters are short runs with one small outlet, so a handful of leaves blocks them completely. They're cleared as part of every standard visit.

### Do I need to be home?

Usually not. If side gates can be left unlocked, the gutters can be cleared and the photos sent afterwards.
