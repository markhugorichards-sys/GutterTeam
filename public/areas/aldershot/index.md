## Which parts of Aldershot are covered?

Aldershot grew quickly after the permanent army camp arrived in 1854. By the 1890s it was a prosperous town of around 25,000 people, and it later grew as a commuter settlement. Its housing is denser than most of the surrounding area. Victorian terraces, garrison-era villas and the large new Wellesley development all sit within a couple of miles.

**Aldershot West**  
Conservation area (designated 1980, reviewed 2025) covering older residential streets west of the town centre.

**Manor Park and Cargate**  
Two further conservation areas, both designated in 1980. Manor Park also gives its name to one of the town's main parks.

**North Town**  
Terraced and semi-detached streets north of the centre. Gutters are often shared between neighbouring houses.

**Wellesley**  
The new neighbourhood on former garrison land between Aldershot and Farnborough. Its modern uPVC systems include three-storey town houses.

## What are Aldershot homes and gutters like?

### Victorian and Edwardian terraces

Dense terraced streets from the town's garrison boom. Rear access is often through a narrow passage, and back additions have their own short gutter runs.

### Post-war rebuilding

The barracks were substantially rebuilt between 1961 and 1969, and the wider town was redeveloped in the 1960s and 1970s.

### New-build

Wellesley's three-storey homes need longer reach equipment, which affects the price more than the gutter length does.

## How do Aldershot’s trees affect your gutters?

The conservation areas and parks such as Manor Park, Brickfields Country Park and Dukes Park put mature broadleaf trees close to housing.

The Blackwater Valley and the Basingstoke Canal mark the eastern and northern edges of the town.

### What problems are most common in Aldershot?

- Back-addition gutters on terraces, which are easy to forget and often the first to overflow
- Shared gutters across terraced houses, where one blocked outlet floods next door's wall
- Any surviving cast iron on period houses, which rusts at the joints if left unpainted

**Best time to book in Aldershot:** Aldershot's terraces are best cleared after leaf fall in late autumn. If you share a run with your neighbour, book together: a clear gutter next to a blocked one still overflows.
