## Which parts of Farnham are covered?

Farnham is often described as one of the finest Georgian towns in England. Hop-growing wealth in the mid-1700s paid for the red-brick houses at the heart of the town, and the town centre conservation area dates from 1970. Hundreds of listed buildings mean many Farnham gutters are traditional cast iron, which needs a gentler, more careful approach than modern uPVC.

**Town centre and Castle Street**  
Georgian and older buildings in the conservation area, often with cast iron gutters and hoppers.

**The Bourne and Moor Park**  
Larger homes in leafy, sloping settings south of the town.

**Hale, Heath End, Weybourne and Badshot Lea**  
Mainly 20th-century housing to the north and east, closer to Aldershot.

**Wrecclesham, Rowledge and Boundstone**  
Village-edge homes to the south-west, with plenty of mature trees.

**Firgrove and North West Farnham**  
Mixed period and later housing close to the town centre.

## How do Farnham’s trees affect your gutters?

The River Wey corridor and the wooded southern settlements around The Bourne and Rowledge bring heavy autumn leaf fall to many Farnham roofs.

The River Wey runs through the town.

### What problems are most common in Farnham?

- Cast iron joints rusting and leaking where paint has failed
- Hoppers and parapet outlets on older buildings, which block easily and are hard to see from the street
- Leaf loads on sloping, tree-lined plots in The Bourne and Moor Park

**Best time to book in Farnham:** Clean in late autumn, and use the visit to check paint condition on cast iron. Repainting is best done in dry spring or summer weather.

## Gutter repairs, replacement and concrete gutters in Farnham

Farnham's listed Georgian core around Castle Street often has cast iron gutters and hoppers, while later houses mostly have uPVC. If your house was built in the 1950s or 1960s it may have concrete gutters cast into the eaves instead. Each needs a different approach.

- **Cast iron.** Repairs are usually like-for-like. In the conservation area, changing to uPVC may need consent from Waverley Borough Council. See the [cast iron gutters guide](https://www.gutterteam.co.uk/advice/cast-iron-gutters/).
- **Concrete gutters.** These are cast into the eaves and tend to leak at the joints. The options are relining, or removing them and fitting fascia boards with standard guttering. See [gutter replacement](https://www.gutterteam.co.uk/services/gutter-replacement/) for typical costs.
- **uPVC.** Leaking joints and slipped brackets are usually a small [repair](https://www.gutterteam.co.uk/services/gutter-repairs/). Brittle, faded runs are better replaced.

Ask for repairs, replacement or new guttering in the enquiry form and the price comes back by email.

## Farnham questions

### My Farnham house is listed. Can the gutters be repaired?

Cleaning and like-for-like repair of cast iron are normally fine. Replacing cast iron with plastic, or changing the profile, on a listed building usually needs listed building consent from Waverley Borough Council. Check before any replacement.

### Is cast iron worth keeping?

Usually, yes. Well-maintained cast iron can last more than a century, and traditional profiles are still made. Keeping it painted and the joints sealed is the main job.

### Do I need to be home?

Usually not. If side gates can be left unlocked, the gutters can be cleared and the photos sent afterwards.
