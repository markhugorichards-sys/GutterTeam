Gutter cleaning in Sandhurst

Sandhurst changed little until the Royal Military College moved here from Marlow in 1812 and the railway arrived in 1849. Large housing estates built from the late 1950s to the 1980s joined Little Sandhurst, central Sandhurst, College Town and Owlsmoor into one town. That town is framed by woods, heaths and fir plantations.

Trees, water and what they mean for your gutters

Edgbarrow Woods and Wildmoor Heath lie to the north, with the fir plantations of Broadmoor Bottom to the east. Mixed broadleaved and coniferous woodland frames much of the town, so expect needles as well as leaves.

The River Blackwater forms the southern boundary, with several of the Yateley Lakes, including Trilakes, inside the parish.

Problems we see most in Sandhurst

  • Pine and fir needle build-up on homes next to the woods and plantations
  • Ageing seals on 1960s to 1980s estate gutters
  • Blocked downpipes from compacted needle mats

Sandhurst questions

Is Sandhurst covered even though it's in Berkshire?

Yes. Sandhurst, College Town, Little Sandhurst and Owlsmoor are all inside the coverage area.

Why do my downpipes keep blocking?

Needles wash into the outlet and compact into a plug. Each visit clears the downpipes and checks that water flows freely, not just the gutter itself.

Do I need to be home?

Usually not. If side gates can be left unlocked, the gutters can be cleared and the photos sent afterwards.

How is the price confirmed?

You describe your home in the form and get a confirmed price by email before anything is booked. If a repair is found during the visit, it is photographed and priced separately, never added without your agreement.
