## Which parts of Yateley are covered?

Yateley is the second-largest urban area in Hart District. It sits on terraces rising south from the River Blackwater, whose valley floor is now largely the flooded gravel workings of the Yateley Lakes. Its historic greens form three conservation areas, ringed by extensive 1960s to 1990s housing, with wooded heath along the southern edge.

**Yateley Green**  
The largest of the greens and the heart of the main conservation area.

**Cricket Hill and Darby Green**  
The other two conservation areas, with older cottages among trees.

**West Yateley and Moulsham Green**  
Mature broadleaf trees around Moulsham Green and estates from the 1960s to the 1990s.

**South Yateley and Vigo Lane**  
A thickly wooded approach on Vigo Lane, a high number of mature broadleaf trees, and wooded heathland to the south.

**East Yateley**  
Mature trees around Potley Hill Road, with 1960s estates and bungalow cul-de-sacs.

## What are Yateley homes and gutters like?

### Historic cottages

16th to 18th-century houses survive around the greens, some with plain tile and even thatch.

### 1960s estates

The bulk of Yateley's housing, mostly with concrete tile roofs, plus many bungalows and chalet bungalows.

### 1970s to 2000s

Later estates such as Blair Park, with standard two-storey uPVC systems.

## How do Yateley’s trees affect your gutters?

Hart's character study notes a high number of mature broadleaf trees in South Yateley, wooded heathland to the south and many mature trees in East Yateley. Many homes get both oak leaf fall and heathland pine.

The River Blackwater and the Yateley Lakes lie along the northern edge of the town.

### What problems are most common in Yateley?

- Moss and grit washing off concrete tile roofs on the 1960s estates
- Chalet bungalow dormer gutters, which are easy to miss
- Leaf and needle loads near the wooded southern edge

**Best time to book in Yateley:** Book a clean in December after leaf fall. Homes near Vigo Lane and the southern woodland should add a spring visit.
