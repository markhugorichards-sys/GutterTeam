## Which parts of Camberley are covered?

Camberley is one of the most tree-covered towns in the area. Surrey Heath's own character study describes 'Wooded Hills' to the east and south dominated by large trees, many of them protected, and some roads forming green tunnels. These wooded estates survive from the 19th-century pine plantations. The result is a lot of pine needles in Camberley gutters.

**Heatherside**  
A 1970s Bovis estate on former heathland, with long winding streets and generous mature trees, including the Wellingtonia Avenue group.

**Old Dean**  
A 1950s estate of plain two-storey semis and terraces with prominent green spaces.

**Edwardian streets west of the centre**  
Victorian, Edwardian and inter-war houses around Grand Avenue and Southwell Park Road, often two and a half storeys.

**The Wooded Hills**  
Postwar and contemporary houses under dense tree cover around Upper Chobham Road and Kings Ride.

**York Town**  
19th-century development along London Road, with Edwardian worker housing and newer terraces.

## What are Camberley homes and gutters like?

### Victorian, Edwardian and inter-war

Often two and a half storeys, with dormers and valley gutters that need checking as well as the main run.

### 1950s to 1970s estates

Old Dean and Heatherside: standard two-storey systems, usually quick to clear.

### Wooded Hills

Detached homes under heavy tree cover, with long runs and constant needle fall.

## How do Camberley’s trees affect your gutters?

Pine is the defining tree here. Needles fall all year rather than in one autumn drop, and they knit into a water-holding mat that passes straight through many gutter guards.

### What problems are most common in Camberley?

- Year-round pine needle build-up in the Wooded Hills and around Heatherside
- Valley and dormer gutters on two-and-a-half-storey period houses
- Clogged gutter guards under pine. Guards need clearing too

**Best time to book in Camberley:** Homes under pine in Camberley often need two or three visits a year. Homes on the more open estates usually need one, in winter.

## Camberley questions

### Do gutter guards stop pine needles?

Not reliably. Needles slip through mesh and lie on top of brush guards, so guards on Camberley homes under pine still need regular checks.
