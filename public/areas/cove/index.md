## Which parts of Cove and Southwood are covered?

Cove is an old village that became the western suburb of Farnborough. Southwood, next to it, is largely a 1980s estate built on a former army camp and radio station site. The Cove and Southwood ward runs from the railway in the north to Elles Road in the south and Marrowbrook Lane in the east.

**Cove village and Cove Road**  
The older core around St John the Baptist church, with a mix of period cottages, inter-war houses and later infill.

**Southwood**  
A 1980s estate of detached, semi-detached and terraced houses. These are mostly uPVC gutters, now around 40 years old, with joints and brackets to match.

**Along Cove Brook**  
Homes backing onto the Cove Brook Greenway get more leaf and seed fall from the brookside trees.

## What are Cove and Southwood homes and gutters like?

### Period cottages and inter-war homes

Found in the older Cove streets. Gutter profiles vary from house to house, so a repair needs matching parts.

### 1980s Southwood estate

Built on the site of Southwood Camp and Cove Radio Station. The housing is uniform, and the original gutter seals are now reaching the end of their life.

## How do Cove and Southwood’s trees affect your gutters?

The Cove Brook Greenway and Southwood Playing Fields bring tree lines close to back gardens. Expect sycamore and other broadleaf seed in spring and leaves in autumn.

Cove Brook runs north through the area as a protected green corridor.

### What problems are most common in Cove and Southwood?

- Dripping joints on original 1980s uPVC systems, usually at the unions closest to the downpipe
- Leaf build-up on homes that back onto the brook corridor
- Shared downpipes between terraced houses

**Best time to book in Cove and Southwood:** A single clean each winter suits most Southwood homes. Homes backing onto the brook trees usually need a second visit in spring.

## Cove and Southwood questions

### Is it worth resealing 1980s gutters or should I replace them?

If the gutter is straight and the brackets are sound, resealing the leaking joints costs far less than a new system. Replacement makes sense when sections have gone brittle or the run sags between brackets.

### Do you cover the whole of Cove and Southwood?
