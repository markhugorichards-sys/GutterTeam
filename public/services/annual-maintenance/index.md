# Annual Maintenance Plans

If your home is under trees or you manage several properties, a scheduled plan takes gutter cleaning off your to-do list. Visits are booked at the right time of year for the trees over each roof, and each visit leaves a dated photo record.

## How a plan works

1. Agree the visit pattern: once a year in winter, or twice a year (winter and spring) for homes under trees
2. Visits are booked in automatically, with a reminder before each one
3. Before and after photos and condition notes are sent after each visit
4. Any repair needed is priced separately, never added automatically

## Who it suits

- Homes under oak, horse chestnut or pine
- Landlords who need a maintenance record for each property
- Older owners who'd rather not chase a tradesperson each year

## Questions

### Can I cancel a plan?

Yes. Plans are rolling and can be stopped at any time.

### Do I need to be home?

Usually not. If side gates can be left unlocked, the gutters can be cleared and the photos sent afterwards.

### How is the price confirmed?

You describe your home in the form and get a confirmed price by email before anything is booked. If a repair is found during the visit, it is photographed and priced separately, never added without your agreement.
