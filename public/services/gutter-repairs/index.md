# Gutter Repairs

Most gutter problems are small and cheap to fix if caught early: a dripping joint, a slipped bracket, a run that has lost its fall. Repairs are priced from photos where possible, and nothing is done without your agreement.

## Common repairs

| Problem | Typical fix |
| --- | --- |
| Dripping joint or union | Clean and reseal, or replace the union |
| Sagging section | Add or replace brackets and reset the fall |
| Cracked or split section | Replace the section with a matching profile |
| Water missing the gutter | Adjust the gutter line or fit a deflector |
| Rusting cast iron joint | Clean back, seal and repaint |

## Matching parts

The area has gutters of every age, from Georgian cast iron in Farnham to 1980s uPVC in Southwood and 2000s systems at Elvetham Heath. Matching the profile (half round, square, ogee or deep flow) and the colour matters. Mismatched parts are a common cause of repeat leaks.

## Listed buildings and conservation areas

Like-for-like repairs don't change the appearance of a building. Changing material or profile on a listed building, or on a front elevation covered by an Article 4 direction, can need consent. Check with your council first. The area pages list the conservation areas in each town.

## Questions

### Repair or replace?

If the run is straight and the brackets are sound, resealing and part replacement costs far less than a new system. Full replacement makes sense when the plastic has gone brittle or several sections are failing.

### Can a repair be priced from photos?

Often, yes. Attach a description in the enquiry form and the reply will say whether photos are enough or a look in person is needed.

### Do I need to be home?

Usually not. If side gates can be left unlocked, the gutters can be cleared and the photos sent afterwards.

### How is the price confirmed?

You describe your home in the form and get a confirmed price by email before anything is booked. If a repair is found during the visit, it is photographed and priced separately, never added without your agreement.
