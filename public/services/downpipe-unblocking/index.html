Downpipe Unblocking

A gutter that overflows at one corner, even when it looks clean, usually has a blocked downpipe. The pipe is cleared from the outlet or the shoe, then flushed so you can see water running freely.

Signs of a blocked downpipe

  • Water spilling over the gutter next to the downpipe outlet during rain
  • Gurgling, or water bubbling out of joints partway down the pipe
  • A damp patch or green staining on the wall behind the pipe
  • Plants growing out of the hopper or the top of the pipe

Common causes locally

Pine needles are the main culprit in Camberley, Sandhurst, Yateley and the heath-side streets. They wash down and compact into a plug. Moss and roof grit from older concrete tile roofs is the next most common cause, followed by tennis balls and bird nests.

What clearing involves

The blockage is located, cleared with rods or a jetting attachment, and the pipe is flushed and flow-tested. If the pipe discharges into an underground drain that is also blocked, you'll be told, because that's usually a drainage job rather than a gutter job.

Questions

Is a blocked downpipe urgent?

It can be. Water pouring down a wall in heavy rain soaks brickwork and can cause damp inside. If you see water coming over the gutter next to the downpipe, get it looked at soon.

Can downpipe unblocking be done at the same visit as a clean?

Yes. Most downpipe blockages are found and cleared during a standard gutter clean.

Do I need to be home?

Usually not. If side gates can be left unlocked, the gutters can be cleared and the photos sent afterwards.

How is the price confirmed?

You describe your home in the form and get a confirmed price by email before anything is booked. If a repair is found during the visit, it is photographed and priced separately, never added without your agreement.
