# Fascia and Soffit Cleaning

Green algae and black streaks on fascias and the outside face of gutters are cosmetic, but they make a house look neglected. A wash at the same visit as a gutter clean is the cheapest way to do it.

## What's cleaned

- uPVC fascia boards and soffits
- The outside face of the gutters and downpipes
- Bargeboards on gable ends

## When it's worth doing

Before selling or letting a property, after scaffolding has come down, or when green growth has built up on the shaded north side of the house.

## What it won't fix

Painted timber fascias that are flaking or soft need repair and redecoration, not washing. Any rotten timber found during the visit is pointed out.

### Can this be done at the same time as gutter cleaning?

Yes, and that's the most cost-effective way. See the price guide for typical add-on costs.

Usually not. If side gates can be left unlocked, the gutters can be cleared and the photos sent afterwards.
