# Gutter Replacement

New uPVC guttering is typically priced at about £20 to £50 per metre supplied and fitted, depending on the guide, and published figures for a whole semi-detached house run from about £400 to £900. Replacement makes sense when several sections are brittle, leaking or the wrong size for the roof. Where only a joint or bracket has failed, a repair costs far less.

## How much does gutter replacement cost?

| Material | Typical supply and fit, per metre |
| --- | --- |
| uPVC (plastic) | £20 to £50 |
| Aluminium | £50 to £78 |
| Cast iron | £50 to £85, and up to £150 for heritage profiles |
| Replacement uPVC downpipe | £35 to £45 |

Whole-house totals for uPVC run from about £400 to £900 on a typical semi, and published UK averages for all materials sit around £1,000 to £1,600. These are market estimates from the guides listed below, not quotes. Your price is confirmed by email before anything is booked.

## When is replacement better than repair?

- **Repair** if the run is straight, the brackets are sound and the leak is at one or two joints. Resealing a joint or fitting a new bracket is a small job.
- **Replace** if the plastic has gone brittle or faded, several joints leak at once, the gutter has lost its fall along the whole run, or the profile is too small for the roof and overflows in heavy rain.

See [gutter repairs](https://www.gutterteam.co.uk/services/gutter-repairs/) for the smaller jobs.

## Which material suits your house?

- **uPVC** suits most post-war houses and is the cheapest to buy and maintain.
- **Cast iron** is common on listed buildings and older houses in conservation areas, such as central Farnham. See the [cast iron gutters guide](https://www.gutterteam.co.uk/advice/cast-iron-gutters/).
- **Aluminium** is lighter than cast iron, does not rust and is available in heritage-style profiles.

## What about concrete gutters?

Some 1950s and 1960s houses, including parts of Farnham and Aldershot, have concrete gutters cast as part of the eaves. They often leak at the joints and can let water into the wall below. The usual choices are relining the concrete gutter, or removing it and fitting a fascia board with standard guttering. Removal is the larger job: MyJobQuote puts the average concrete gutter replacement at about £2,400 in 2026.

## Do I need permission to change my gutters?

Like-for-like replacement does not normally need consent. Changing the material, colour or profile on a listed building, or on a front elevation covered by an Article 4 direction, may. Check with your council first. Each [area page](https://www.gutterteam.co.uk/areas/) lists the conservation areas in that town.

## Questions

### How long does gutter replacement take?

A typical house is usually done in a day. Larger houses, cast iron systems and concrete gutter conversions can take longer, and the timing is confirmed with your price.

### Can new gutters be fitted to old fascias?

Only if the fascia boards are sound. Soft or rotten timber is pointed out before fitting, because new brackets will not hold in it.

### Is uPVC allowed on a period house?

On most houses, yes. On a listed building, or a front elevation covered by an Article 4 direction, changing from cast iron to uPVC may need consent. Check with your council.

### Do I need to be home?

Usually not. If side gates can be left unlocked, the gutters can be cleared and the photos sent afterwards.
